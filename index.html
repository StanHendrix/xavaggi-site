About

THE DJ

A 25-year-old DJ from Venlo. Raised among the parties, the fields and the noise.

It started at friends' parties and never really stopped. Xavaggi doesn't pin himself to one side. Hard when it needs to be, melodic when it can be, the same pull that runs through his Chiaroscuro mix series.

Data scientist by day, DJ the rest of the time. On the dancefloor or behind the bar, it doesn't matter where, as long as the music is right.
